## Route And Scope

The development backend generates immutable Music, BGMList, PianoScoreInfo and
PianoScore packages, mounts genuine PackageStore metadata, retains independent
DataObject acquisitions and registers separate rows. A selected full FName binds
the game's ordinary audio and chart lookup to those resources. No source-field
replacement or playback replay is needed. The earlier controlled prototype is
documented in [Native Cached Audio Evidence](NativeCachedAudioEvidence-20261016.md).

Runtime-gate session IDs below refer to external immutable collections and manual
receipts. Logs are not repository links or standalone source dependencies. Failed
development candidates were exited normally and rolled back; session-created
decoder companions were removed only after closure and exact recorded-hash checks.
No shared loader configuration was changed.
